General Safety Tips

  • 💰 During Robbery: Stay calm, don’t resist, and call police immediately.
  • 🙅 Harassment: Use SOS alert and move to nearest safe zone.
  • 🩺 Medical Emergency: Call 108 and inform nearby people.
  • 🛂 Lost Passport: Report to police & nearest embassy quickly.
  • 📱 Always keep phone charged & share your live location with trusted contacts.
  • 🚖 Verify cab/driver details before boarding. Prefer official apps.

Fire Safety

  • Install smoke detectors on every floor and test monthly.
  • Plan and practice an escape route; keep exits clear.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher accessible and know how to use it (PASS).
  • Avoid overloaded outlets and unplug unused appliances.
  • Store flammable materials safely and away from heat sources.

Flood Safety

  • Know your area's flood risk and have an evacuation plan.
  • Keep important documents waterproofed and elevated.
  • Move to higher ground immediately if flood warnings are issued.
  • Do not walk or drive through floodwaters — 6 inches can knock you down.
  • Shut off electricity and gas if instructed by authorities.

Earthquake Safety

  • Drop, cover and hold on — protect your head and neck.
  • Secure heavy furniture and mount shelves to studs.
  • Create a family communication and reunification plan.
  • Keep an emergency kit with water, food, flashlight and batteries.
  • After shaking stops, check for hazards (gas leaks, structural damage).

General Emergency Safety

  • Assemble a 72-hour emergency kit (water, food, meds, tools).
  • Keep a list of emergency contacts and local emergency numbers.
  • Learn basic first aid and CPR.
  • Stay informed via battery/hand-crank radio or phone alerts.
  • Practice situational awareness and avoid risky areas after an incident.
